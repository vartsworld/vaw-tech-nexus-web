Core Position

Understanding Payment Gateways vs. Remittance Rails

Using a 3rd-party provider for cross-border money movement drastically reduces engineering complexity and compliance risk. However, not all payment tools do the same job.

Collection Only

Stripe

Great at collecting card payments in the UK, but does not convert GBP to INR and pay out to Indian bank accounts directly. International card fees (approx. 4.3% + 2% FX) make it unfeasible as an end-to-end remittance rail.

Payout Only

RazorpayX

Excellent for payouts to Indian bank accounts and UPI IDs over IMPS/NEFT. However, it requires money to already be inside India in INR. It does not handle UK collection, FX, or cross-border transfers.

End-to-End Rail

Wise Platform

Purpose-built for end-to-end international transfers. Handles GBP collection, live FX conversion, compliance checks, and instant INR payout to Indian bank accounts and UPI IDs within a single API suite.

The Compliance Reality

Cobbling together Stripe (collection) + corporate bank transfer + RazorpayX (payout) creates a self-managed money transmitter with heavy legal liabilities. Choosing an integrated rail like Wise ensures that the regulated cross-border leg is handled entirely by a licensed institution.

Building for Provider Independence

To avoid vendor lock-in, we integrate Wise behind an Adapter Layer. If pricing changes or business requirements expand, adding a secondary rail like Nium requires zero changes to the core mobile apps or internal systems.

Architecture Design

Decoupled & Future-Proof

We build your platform using the Adapter Pattern. Your applications communicate with our standardized internal API, which then communicates with Wise.

The Adapter Layer

Our system uses clean internal commands: getQuote, addRecipient, sendTransfer, and getTransferStatus. A dedicated Wise Adapter translates these into Wise API calls.

Business Benefits

  • No Vendor Lock-In: Switch or add payment providers easily in the future.
  • Negotiating Power: Maintain strong commercial leverage during provider renewals.
  • Zero Mobile App Disruption: Backend rail changes require zero updates to customer mobile apps.
