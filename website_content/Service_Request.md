## 2. Conversational Form Structure (Multi-step)
The service request features an interactive, modern conversational multi-step form to collect client briefs efficiently.

### Reordered Steps & Prompts

#### Step 1: Personal Info
- **Prompt 1**: What's your full name?
  - *Input type*: Text (Placeholder: "John Doe")
- **Prompt 2**: What's your company name? (optional)
  - *Input type*: Text (Placeholder: "Acme Inc.")
- **Prompt 3**: What's your phone number?
  - *Input type*: Phone/Tel (Placeholder: "+1 (555) 123-4567")

#### Step 2: Service Selection
- **Prompt**: Which services are you interested in? (Choose one or more)
- **Checkboxes/Options List**:
  - Website Development
  - WebApp Development
  - AI Solutions
  - VR/AR Development
  - Digital Marketing
  - Digital Design
  - Digital Album
  - NFC/Digital Business Card
  - Digital Poster/Ads Design
  - E-book/Catalogs
  - Custom Software
  - App/Platform
  - AI Platform/AI Character Chatbots
  - Custom VR/AR Projects

#### Step 3: Contact Info
- **Prompt**: What's your email address?
  - *Input type*: Email (Placeholder: "john@example.com")

#### Step 4: Date of Birth
- **Prompt**: When were you born?
  - *Input type*: Calendar / Date picker

#### Step 5: Address Information
- **Prompt 1**: Address Line 1
  - *Input type*: Text / Textarea (Placeholder: "123 Main St")
- **Prompt 2**: City
  - *Input type*: Text (Placeholder: "New York")
- **Prompt 3**: State/Province
  - *Input type*: Text (Placeholder: "NY")
- **Prompt 4**: Pin/Postal Code
  - *Input type*: Text (Placeholder: "10001")
- **Prompt 5**: Country
  - *Input type*: Select dropdown
  - *Available Countries*: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, India, Germany, France, Japan, China, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Singapore, United Arab Emirates.

#### Step 6: Brand Logo (Optional)
- **Prompt**: Do you have a logo to upload? (optional)
  - *Input type*: File upload (Accepts PNG, JPG, SVG up to 5MB)

#### Step 7: Summary & Submit
- **Display**: Displays complete summarized text of full name, company name, phone, email, DOB, selected services, full address details, and logo file name.
- **Actions**: "Back" to correct information or "Submit" to save the request into the database.
- **Terms**: "Please review your information before submitting. You can go back to edit if needed."
