# Intern Experience Page Content

## SEO & Metadata
- **Title**: Share Your Intern Experience
- **Description**: Provide your feedback and share your journey as a VAW Technologies intern. Your insights help us improve and allow us to issue your completion certificate.

---

## 1. Header Information
- **Heading**: Share Your Internship Experience
- **Subtitle**: Help us improve our program and get your completion certificate by sharing your valuable feedback.

---

## 2. Intern Experience Form Fields
Interns submit their program reviews through the following questionnaire:

- **Full Name***: Text box
- **Email***: Email input
- **Phone Number**: Optional text box
- **Internship Domain***: Selection dropdown (UI/UX Designing, AI Training, Data Labeling, Prompt Engineering, Web Development, App Development, Graphic Design, Digital Marketing, Content Creation)
- **Address**: Complete physical address for certificate delivery
- **Overall Experience Rating***: Interactive 1-to-5 star rating
- **Overall Experience Essay***: "Share Your Overall Experience" (Min 50 characters, covering liked aspects, challenges faced, etc.)
- **Skills You Learned**: What new skills or technologies did you learn during the internship? (Textarea)
- **Project Highlights**: What projects did you work on and what were your key contributions? (Textarea)
- **Mentor Feedback**: Feedback on the mentoring experience and mentors (Textarea)
- **Suggestions for Improvement**: Recommendations on how to improve the internship program (Textarea)
- **Recommendation Opt-in**: Checkbox to recommend this internship to others
- **Certificate Request Opt-in**: Checkbox to request the physical & online completion certificate

---

## 3. Success & Certificate Issuance Details
Once submitted, the system displays congratulations and certificate delivery details:

- **Certificate Terms**:
  - Internship completion certificate is issued within **7 days**.
  - Recieved online via email.
  - Physical copy delivered to the provided address.
  - Officially recognized for academic/professional purposes.

- **Option to join VAW Team**:
  - Button: "Join the VAW Team with Training" (opens Dialog to apply directly to join the core team).
